# fid29-dressing: assets and licences

Every third-party file the dressing lane fetched, where from, under what licence, and what ships. All were fetched on
14 Nov 2026 to `mm1:~/cq-overflow/b29/dressing/dl/` (CPU host; nothing over 1 GB, about 71 MB in all) by
`tools/props` scripts (`ph_dl.py`, a fixed User-Agent as the Poly Haven API terms ask). Nothing fetched is shipped as
downloaded: each prop is decimated in Blender (CPU, mm1), its maps packed into one atlas per tier, and every albedo was
inspected for logos, stickers, maker plates and text (checklist in `dressing.md`).
